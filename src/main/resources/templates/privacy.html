gradegoal Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 7, 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us. Please read this policy carefully.

Welcome to gradegoal ("we," "us," "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our web application. By using gradegoal, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

We comply with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR for EEA users and the CCPA for California residents.

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, password, and academic role (student/teacher).
  • Academic Data:
    • Marks and Grades: Course names, assignments, test scores, and calculated GPAs.
    • Weekly Timetable: Information about recurring classes, including course names, times, and locations you enter.
    • Academic Goals: GPA targets, course mark goals, and other personal academic objectives you set within the app.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, features used, and timestamps.
  • Device Information: Operating system, device type, and unique device identifiers.

We do not collect sensitive payment information directly; any payments are processed by third-party payment processors.

We use the information we collect for:

  • Providing the Service: To calculate GPAs, display your timetable, track progress toward goals, and personalize your experience.
  • Improving the Service: To understand usage patterns, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • Communication: To send service updates, respond to your inquiries, and (with your consent) share tips or promotional content.
  • Research and Analytics: To generate anonymized, aggregated statistics (e.g., average GPA trends) that do not identify you.
  • Security and Compliance: To protect against unauthorized access, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only in these circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly direct us to share data (e.g., with a teacher or study group).
  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics). They are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Educational Institutions: If you are using gradegoal through a school affiliation, we may share relevant academic data with authorized personnel at that institution.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred with notice to you.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data from active databases within 30 days, though anonymized aggregates may be retained. You can export your data at any time from your account settings.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
